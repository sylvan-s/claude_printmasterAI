# Fine Art Print Analyzer — App Store Description

**Subtitle**: AI-Powered Cataloging, Appraisal, and Analysis for Fine Art Prints & Multiples

---

### 🎨 Discover the Story Behind Every Print

Unlock the secrets of your art collection with **Fine Art Print Analyzer**, the premier automated cataloging and appraisal companion built for collectors, galleries, curators, and auction specialists. Powered by advanced Gemini AI, this application transforms high-resolution scans and photos of prints, lithographs, engravings, and woodblocks into academic-grade catalog records and valuations in seconds.

Whether you are a seasoned prints dealer managing large auction lots or an art enthusiast cataloging your personal collection, Fine Art Print Analyzer streamlines your workflow, bringing elite connoisseurship directly to your fingertips.

---

### 🚀 Key Features

#### 🔍 Elite AI-Powered Print Appraisals
- **Multi-Angle Visual Analysis**: Upload a primary scan of your print along with close-up highlights (such as signatures, stamps, paper texture, watermarks, or damage).
- **Academic Attribution**: Receive instant, highly reliable predictions for the artist, title, creation period, and visual movement.
- **Valuation & Estimates**: Get automatic real-time auction estimates (low and high estimates in your preferred currency) backed by historical cataloging context.

#### 📦 Automatic Multi-Artwork Detection
- **Instant Collage Segmentation**: Scanned multiple sheets on a single flatbed scanner? Our intelligent bounding-box detection automatically identifies and segments separate print sheets from a single image.
- **Coordinate Tracking**: Instantly crop and isolate individual works from a composite photo with zero effort.

#### 🏛️ Smart Lot Structuring & Cataloging
- **Scholarly Lot Proposals**: Select multiple prints from your inventory, and let Gemini analyze their shared artists, techniques, visual schools, or movements to propose a cohesive, professionally titled auction lot (e.g., *"Selected Woodblock Prints of the Shin-hanga Movement"*).
- **Custom Catalog Management**: Group your prints into logical collections, rename catalogs dynamically, and manage your inventory with responsive client-side caching.

#### 🔬 Flexible Appraisal Methods
- **Tailored Analysis Models**: Switch between Standard, Simplified, or Strict appraisal templates. 
- **Admin Prompt Editor**: Customize system instructions, temperature, and visual settings to fine-tune Gemini's analysis for specific genres or historical periods.

#### 🔒 Enterprise-Grade Privacy & Security
- **Row-Level Security (RLS)**: Your private collections and personal data are protected by hardened database security protocols.
- **Complete Data Control**: Easily manage your account credentials, or wipe your appraisal history and uploaded assets with full data-privacy compliance.

### 👥 Who is Fine Art Print Analyzer For?

*   **Art Collectors**: Document, evaluate, and track the value of your private collection.
*   **Galleries & Dealers**: Instantly draft cataloging listings and verify techniques for prints on paper.
*   **Auction Houses**: Group incoming consignments into cohesive, academic auction lots with proposed titles and lot numbers.
